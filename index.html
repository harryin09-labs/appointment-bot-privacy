Privacy Policy

WhatsApp Automation & Appointment Scheduling

1. Introduction

Automexa provides automation solutions, including WhatsApp-based appointment scheduling, customer communication, and workflow integrations.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, shared, stored, and deleted through Automexa-operated services, including the n8nCustSuppBot application.

Some appointment services are operated directly by Automexa. Other services are provided to independent clinics and businesses.

Important: When an independent clinic uses Automexa, that clinic may determine how patient and appointment information is handled. Its own privacy notice may also apply.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the service and information provided, the following categories may be processed:

  • Full name and contact information.
  • WhatsApp phone number and message identifiers.
  • Appointment dates, times, and booking IDs.
  • Appointment cancellation and rescheduling records.
  • Messages submitted through the appointment assistant.
  • Service requests and relevant communication records.
  • Technical information needed for workflow operation.

Users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information through WhatsApp, including medical records or detailed health information, unless the clinic specifically requires and appropriately protects that information.

3. How Information Is Collected

  • When users contact a WhatsApp assistant.
  • When users request or modify appointments.
  • When a clinic submits appointment information.
  • When connected platforms provide service data.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed for these purposes:

  • Creating and confirming appointments.
  • Checking calendar availability.
  • Rescheduling or cancelling appointments.
  • Identifying appointments linked to a user.
  • Responding to service-related messages.
  • Maintaining appointment activity records.
  • Preventing unauthorized changes to bookings.
  • Troubleshooting and maintaining automation services.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

Processing is carried out where permitted by applicable law, including on the basis of consent, service-related necessity, or other applicable legal grounds.

5. WhatsApp and Meta Platforms

Automexa uses the WhatsApp Business Platform to support automated communications.

Messages and related technical information are processed through Meta's infrastructure in accordance with its applicable terms and privacy practices.

WhatsApp communications may include appointment confirmations, responses to customer requests, and other permitted service notifications.

Read WhatsApp's Privacy Policy

6. Third-Party Integrations

Automexa's appointment workflows may use the following services:

  • Google Calendar: Appointment availability and scheduling.
  • Google Sheets: Appointment records and activity logs.
  • n8n: Workflow execution and automation processing.
  • Meta WhatsApp Business Platform: Customer messaging and communication.

Data shared with these services is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the configured workflow.

7. Independent Clinics and Businesses

Automexa may provide appointment automation to independent clinics or service providers.

In these arrangements, the clinic may be the primary party responsible for determining why and how customer information is processed. Automexa may process information on the clinic's instructions as its service provider.

Customers should consult their clinic's privacy notice regarding its appointment records, patient information, and retention practices.

8. Information Sharing

Personal information may be shared with:

  • The clinic responsible for an appointment.
  • Approved providers supporting automation services.
  • Authorities where legally required.

Automexa does not sell personal information as part of its appointment scheduling services.

9. Data Storage and Retention

Appointment information may be stored in connected Google Calendar accounts, Google Sheets, workflow infrastructure, or other systems configured for the relevant service.

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain appropriate records, handle disputes, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Specific retention periods may depend on the relevant clinic's policies and contractual arrangements. Customers may request information about the applicable retention period using the contact details below.

10. Information Security

Automexa aims to apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information being processed.

These may include access restrictions, credential management, protected connections, and appropriate administrative controls.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible information.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Raise questions or complaints about processing.

Some requests may need to be handled by the clinic responsible for the appointment. Legal recordkeeping requirements may limit certain requests.
